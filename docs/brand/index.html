Joshua Tree

A computer you can read end to end. This page is the brand in one place: the mark, the colours, the type and the pieces of the desktop.

The mark

One Joshua tree, painted. Gouache and watercolour in the manner of a wildlife plate: a shaggy bark trunk leaning left, three arms, a spiky rosette at each tip, and a wash of terracotta soil with a few stones. It keeps its own colours on every page, light or dark. The source is docs/brand/tree.png; every copy comes from tools/gen/gen_brand_art.py.

The glyph

Below 48 px the brushwork turns to mud, so the painting is traced flat: the tree in bark ink, the soil in terracotta, the same pose. The favicon, menu bars and anything 32 px or smaller use it. Never use the glyph large where the painting fits.

Colour

Paper, ink and one terracotta, each taken from the painting: its paper, its darkest bark, its soil. The accent is used sparingly: focus rings, links, the one button. Never purple, never teal, never a gradient that changes hue.

The wallpaper

Joshua Tree National Park at dusk, painted soft: rose and violet ridges, backlit trees and a quiet middle for the dock. It is the Pi's default since 13.27, and the landing and the README use it too. The source is art/wallpaper/park-dusk.jpg. tools/gen/gen_park_wall.py turns it into arch/arm64/park_wall.h: 960 by 540 in 256 colours, scaled up smoothly with no scanlines. Settings can still pick a flat colour.

Type

Icons

Painted, not drawn

Each dock icon is a small painting on a soft rounded tile, in the spirit of Surenix's Ayeris: real colour, a little depth, no clutter.

One light, from the top

A soft highlight across the top, the tile a touch darker below, and no dark outline. iconlight-check.py reads it on the real dock.

No words

No text, letters or wordmarks on any icon. No emblem, no speech bubble, no purple or teal.

Window and dock

One frame for every window: an 18 px corner, a hairline under the title, the name centred in ink, a red close dot on the left. The dock is a warm cream tray with a soft shadow. Behind them is the park at dusk.

Notes

Groceries

Bread, eggs, coffee, a new SD card for the Pi.

What it never does

  • Shows a pixel by accident. Text, icons and the wallpaper are all smooth at 2x.
  • Draws an emoji.
  • Uses purple or teal in an icon or the window chrome.
  • Puts a border, stripe or card edge on something just to decorate it.
  • Animates windows open, closed or into a snap.
  • Turns the wallpaper black and white, or night toward black or blue.
  • Uses serif or monospace outside a shell grid.
  • Adds a second accent colour.

Mark history

The mark grew one small step at a time, until 8.0 traded the crayon for a painting.

  • 1.3The engraved badge traced to clean lines; window buttons, a power-symbol sun and a prompt added.
  • 1.4New mark: the engraved tree on its own, a ground line, one ink colour.
  • 2.0The engraved look reaches the favicon, the boot splash and the landing seal.
  • 2.12The same tree finished more carefully: rounded joints, alternating blades, crowns that stay separate at 32 px.
  • 2.25A new, much simpler mark: one tree scribbled in crayon like a hand-drawn album cover.
  • 3.0The crayon tree tightened: steadier lines, denser tufts, and the ground in terracotta, its first colour.
  • 4.0One more crayon stroke at the foot of the trunk. From here the mark is touched with every major release.
  • 8.0A painting replaces the crayon: one Joshua tree in gouache, like a wildlife album cover, with a flat traced glyph for small sizes. Paper, ink and soil come from it.
