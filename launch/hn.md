Title: Show HN: Joshua Tree – an OS from scratch that runs in a browser and on a Pi 4

URL: https://github.com/nulljosh/joshuatree

Text: I wrote a small operating system from the bootloader up, in C with no libc: its own kernel, scheduler, FAT driver, network stack, windowing desktop and about 30 apps. It boots in the browser at joshuatree.jaybulb.com, and an ARM64 port runs on a Raspberry Pi 4 from one flashed card. This week the Pi got accounts with PBKDF2 password hashes, a login screen and sudo. Every change runs a QEMU test suite in CI before it lands. Happy to answer anything about how it works.
