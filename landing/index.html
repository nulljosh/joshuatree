What you just booted

No macOS, no Linux underneath it. I'm writing this one myself,
piece by piece, toward my own file explorer and browser.

Yes, it's a command prompt today. That's honestly where it is — no graphics yet (v6), no conversation with it yet (v10). I'd rather show you the real thing than fake the future version.

Named Joshua Tree, renamed from a plain "os" — a tree that survives on almost nothing in the Mojave, built the same way this kernel is: piece by piece, nothing borrowed. A small nod to the U2 album too. And yeah, my own name.
