# Joshua Tree Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 2026*

Joshua Tree is an operating system. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What leaves your device
- **The OS.** Joshua Tree itself sends nothing anywhere. Network apps inside it only reach the sites you open.
- **AI.** The assistant talks to a local model you run yourself.
- **Site analytics.** The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites. This applies to the website only.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Joshua Tree isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
